Construction glossary

Plain-English meanings of the terms you will hear.

G+1 / G+2 / G+3

Ground floor plus 1, 2 or 3 upper floors. A G+2 building has a ground floor and two floors above it.

RCC

Reinforced Cement Concrete - concrete strengthened with steel bars. Used for foundations, columns, beams and slabs.

BBMP

Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike - the civic body that approves building plans in Bengaluru.

Sanctioned plan

A building plan approved by the local authority, which your construction must follow.

Footing

The base of a column that spreads the building load safely into the soil.

Plinth beam

A beam built at plinth level, tying columns together and supporting the walls above the foundation.

Plinth height

The height of the ground floor level above the finished ground or road level.

Column

A vertical RCC member that carries the load of the building down to the footing.

Beam

A horizontal RCC member that carries the slab load to the columns.

Slab

The flat RCC floor or roof of each level.

Lintel

A beam placed over doors and windows to carry the wall above them.

PCC

Plain Cement Concrete - a lean concrete layer laid under footings to give a clean, level base.

Curing

Keeping concrete moist after casting so it gains full strength.

TMT steel

Thermo-Mechanically Treated steel bars used as reinforcement in RCC (for example Fe-500 / Fe-550 grades).

Setback

The open space that must be left between the building and the plot boundary as per rules.

FAR / FSI

Floor Area Ratio - the ratio of total built-up floor area to the plot area allowed by the authority.

Built-up area

The total constructed area of all floors, including walls and balconies.

Carpet area

The usable floor area inside the walls of a home, excluding walls.

BOQ

Bill of Quantities - an itemised list of materials, quantities and work used to prepare an estimate.

MEP

Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing - the services inside a building.

Waterproofing

Treatment of roofs, bathrooms and other wet areas to prevent seepage.

Anti-termite treatment

A chemical treatment of the soil and foundation to prevent termite damage.

Snag list

A list of small defects or unfinished items noted during the final inspection before handover.

Khata

The record that identifies a property with the local authority in Bengaluru (for example A-khata / B-khata).

Occupancy certificate

A document from the authority confirming that the building was built as per the sanctioned plan and can be occupied.
