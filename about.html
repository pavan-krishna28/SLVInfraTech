About SLV Infra Tech

Founder-led residential construction in South Bengaluru.

Our Story

Quality is not only our standard. It is our identity.

SLV Infra Tech was founded in 2022 by Punith Venkatesh and Venkatesh S with one purpose — to bring honest, high-quality residential construction to the homes of South Bengaluru.

From our first project in Chikkegowdanapalya to our active sites in Thurahalli and Gubbalala, every brick carries that founding promise.

Our Team

Our team is always at your service.

Led by two experienced construction directors who personally oversee every project — from structural design through final handover. No middlemen. No surprises.
