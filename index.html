Our Story

Quality is not only
our standard.

SLV Infra Tech was founded in 2022 by Punith Venkatesh and Venkatesh S with one purpose — to bring honest, high-quality residential construction to the homes of South Bengaluru. From our first project in Chikkegowdanapalya to our active sites in Thurahalli and Gubbalala, every brick carries that founding promise.
