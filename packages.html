FAQ

Packages — frequently asked

What is the difference between the five package tiers?

The construction process and supervision are the same in every tier. What changes is the grade of materials and the brands and fittings used. Your written estimate lists them for your project.

How does pricing work for my area?

Rates are indicative for South Bengaluru and are confirmed after we review your plot, floors and plan.

What is included in the built-up rate versus stilt / parking?

The per sq.ft. rate applies to built-up living floors. Stilt and parking areas are quoted separately in your estimate.

Can I customise a package or mix specifications across tiers?

Yes. A package is a transparent baseline. Material upgrades and selective finish swaps can be quoted before you sign.

How are payments linked to construction?

Payments follow construction stages and are agreed in writing before work begins.

What is not included in the package price?

Typically land, registration and government fees, furniture and appliances. Your written estimate lists exactly what is included.

How accurate is the Instant Cost Estimator?

It gives an indicative total from built-up area × tier rate. Your formal quote is prepared after a plot and plan review.
